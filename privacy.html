Privacy notice

How we handle the information you give us when you report an issue.

What we collect

  • Whether you are an owner or tenant, your name, block, unit number and phone number
  • Details of the issue: category, location and description
  • Any photos or videos you attach

Why we collect it

Only to record, follow up and resolve your complaint, to contact you about it, and to let you check its status.

Who can see it

Only the management office and the management committee members who handle complaints. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, unless required by law.

Where it is stored

In the management's private Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive). It is not publicly accessible. The "Track my case" page only shows the status, category and location of a case, and only to someone who enters both the case ID and the matching phone number.

How long we keep it

Records are kept for up to 2 years after a case is resolved, then deleted.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data you submitted, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the management office. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot continue handling your complaint.

This notice is provided in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
